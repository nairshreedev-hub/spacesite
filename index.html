How do rockets actually work?

Rockets work by the cooperation of many things, such as the combustion of LOX (Liquid Oxygen) and a liquid fuel such as hydrolox, kerolox, or methalox, But in this section we will cover some simple rocketry. The first thing we will cover is gravity turns, this may appear like a complicated thing rocket Science but the idea is actually very simple! A gravity turn is a maneuver that every rocket does to reach an orbit, it is when a rocket tilts at an angle to get both the required altitude, (e.g 150km) and the required horizontal momentum (around 7.7 - 8 km/s), this method is way more efficent than going straight up and turning 90° to reach orbit.
